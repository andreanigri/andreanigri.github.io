Research Overview

Andrea Nigri is an Associate Professor at the Department of Social Sciences, University of Foggia. Prior to joining the University of Foggia, he was a Research Fellow at the Department of Social and Political Sciences, Bocconi University.

He works on developing and improving statistical methodology in the field of Statistical Demography and Longevity Risk. He has an interdisciplinary background with training in Statistics and its application to demographic research, obtained through a Ph.D. at Sapienza University of Rome (Department of Statistics), and through the European Doctoral School of Demography (EDSD), a pan-European Ph.D. training program held at the Max Planck Institute for Demographic Research and the University of Southern Denmark.

Prior to starting the Ph.D. program, in 2016 he was a Research Fellow at the Local Health Department of Padua ULSS6, working on epidemiologic surveillance of asbestos-related disease and occupational exposure.

Research Agenda & Themes

His research serves as a bridge between longevity analysis and statistical learning:

  • Statistical Learning & Deep Learning for Mortality Modeling: Introducing regression trees, neural networks (such as LSTM architectures), and skew-normal mixture models as integrations or full replacements of traditional mortality models (e.g. Lee-Carter extensions).
  • Indirect Estimation of Vital Rates: Developing cutting-edge methods to disaggregate death rates of age-groups and estimate age-specific mortality profiles from summary demographic measures (such as life expectancy at birth).
  • Causes-of-Death & Lifespan Inequality: Investigating causes-of-death transitions, lifespan variation among populations with specific medical conditions, and novel lifespan disparity metrics such as the Average Uneven Mortality index (\(e^\dagger\)).
